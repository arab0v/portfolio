Starting This Blog

2026-09-29 · meta

I'm adding a blog to my portfolio to write about the backend work I actually do: NestJS monorepos, FastAPI services, Postgres/Redis tradeoffs, and the occasional AI-agent workflow experiment.

Why

Most of what I learn day-to-day at Rezefy, or from past work at Qawn and MedWrite.ai, is worth writing down before I forget it. This is that place.

What to expect

  • Notes on NestJS monorepo structure and turbo pipelines
  • Postgres/Redis performance notes
  • Keycloak auth integration gotchas
  • Occasional posts on AI agent orchestration for dev teams
